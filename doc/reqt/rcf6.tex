Requirement Elicitation:

\begin{tabular}{|p{211pt}|p{211pt}|} \hline
Short name: Spontaneous Fission Data & Control number: 6\\ \hline
Stakeholder: Randy Baker & Request date: October 22, 2004\\
\end{tabular}
\begin{tabular}{|p{433pt}|} \hline
Description: \\ Provide spontaneous fission neutron production
rate (SFR) and normalized spontaneous fission neutron energy spectra
(SFS) on our standard energy group structure for each isotope in the
library.  Accurate data needed for important isotopes: $^{235,238}$U, $^{239,240}$Pu, $^{252}$Cf. \\
\hline
\end{tabular} 
\begin{tabular}{|p{211pt}|p{211pt}|} \hline
Urgent: Unknown & Comment: Initial attempts at contacting
end-users unsuccessful. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{12pt}

\begin{tabular}{|p{433pt}|} \hline
Size: $(\mathtt{num\_grps} + 1) \times \mathtt{sizeof(double)}$ per
isotope; \\ 
Speed: Accessed at host initialization, efficient
execution not required; \\ 
Accuracy: unknown. \\ \hline 
Model: SOURCES 4C code from RSICC Code Package CC-661 \\ \hline 
Response: SFR - real($\frac{\#}{\mathrm{s}\cdot\mathrm{g}}$); \\ 
SFS - real array of size \texttt{num\_grps}; $0
\le \chi_g \le 1$, $\sum_g \chi_g = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} 1 &
\mbox{if fissionable} \\ 0 & \mbox{otherwise} \end{array} \right.$. \\
\hline 
Representation: SFR - real; \\ 
SFS - real array of size \texttt{num\_grps} or parameterized analytic
description. \\ \hline 
Query: What is the SFR for specified isotope?
\\ What is the SFS for a specified isotope on a standard group
structure?\\ \hline T-16 evaluations: E. F. Shores (X--5),
D. G. Madland (T--16)\\ \hline Exception handling: Must return
reasonable data for all isotopes; zero for non-fissionables \\ \hline
Attachments: See Table~\ref{table:sf} \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\vspace{24pt}
