Short name: MG scattering distribution for Monte Carlo & Control number: 15\\ \hline
Stakeholder: Steven Nolen & Request date: March 07, 2005\\
\end{tabular}
\begin{tabular}{|p{433pt}|} \hline
Description: \\ 
Provide Multigroup Scattering Data Support for Monte Carlo through one
of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Addition of high order Legendre polynomial expansion scattering
data that can be converted by the user to equiprobable discrete
cosine scattering data.
\item Addition of routines to convert Legendre polynomial expansion
scattering data to equiprobable discrete cosine scattering data
\item Addition of equiprobable discrete cosine scattering data to data
files
\end{enumerate}
\\ \hline
\end{tabular} 
\begin{tabular}{|p{211pt}|p{211pt}|} \hline
Urgent: No & Comment: \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{12pt}

\begin{tabular}{|p{433pt}|} \hline
Data: 
\begin{enumerate}
\item High order Legendre polynomial expansion scattering data
\item Possibly none
\item Equiprobable discrete cosine expansion scattering data
\end{enumerate} \\ \hline

\end{enumerate} \\ \hline
